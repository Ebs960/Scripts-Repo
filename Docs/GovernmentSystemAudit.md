## Cross-system findings

- **Inactive configuration fixed:** every disabled institution now has zero seats and no veto domains. Elder Council, Republic, Oligarchy, Theocracy, Merchant Republic, Confederate Republic, Federal Republic, and Parliamentary Democracy now use appropriately named institutions. Democracy deliberately uses elections without a council; representation and legislature are independent concepts.
- **Balance outliers normalized:** Chiefdom's +100% melee became +10%; Feudal Monarchy's +50% gold became +12%; Merchant Republic's +55% gold became +20% plus route/trade mechanics; Empathic Union's +100% culture became +20%; Theocracy's +50% faith became +25%.
- **Policy access:** the matrix lists every direct `requiredGovernments` GUID. Policies without entries are broadly or structurally gated. Existing automatic revocation on government loss remains intact.
- **Governor/lord/faction effects:** serialized opinion reactions remain visible on each asset, while institutional modifiers now create systemic autonomy, unrest, administration and council consequences. Some old universal reactions remain and should eventually be migrated to filtered reactions as more personality archetypes become available.
- **Vassals:** required vassal counts are shown. Feudal and autonomy identities use distance/administration mechanics; the current SubjectManager does not expose a data-driven tribute multiplier, so no parallel bespoke tribute implementation was added.
- **Unique content:** direct government GUID matches are listed per row, including shared family content where an asset accepts multiple governments. Existing leader/civilization unique content and unlock chains were not deleted.
- **Prerequisite concern:** several assets still rely on culture assets alone or their unlock provider rather than duplicating a tech gate in GovernmentData. The validator reports broken references and impossible numeric requirements but does not treat an empty direct gate as inherently invalid because unlock lists are themselves structural gates.
